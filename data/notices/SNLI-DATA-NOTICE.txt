The Stanford NLP project page states that the Stanford Natural Language
Inference Corpus by the Stanford NLP Group is licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . The archive contains no
separate license file; the license statement is on the official project page.
The archive README requests citation of the SNLI paper and describes its
sources. It says the premise captions come from Flickr30k except where noted;
Flickr30k is also released under an Attribution-ShareAlike license. The README
also notes that about 4,000 training sentences came from a VisualGenome pilot.
That training-only note does not imply those rows were included in this pilot.

Processing: only labeled development pairs with a consensus gold label are
eligible; rows with gold_label "-" are excluded. Pairs are deduplicated by
whitespace-normalized, case-folded premise and hypothesis. Connected source
groups use captionID or normalized premise links, including links contributed
by duplicate rows. Thirty-two groups are selected by the frozen hash recipe.
Each pair is presented as a three-way relation decision with option IDs in
the original entailment, neutral, contradiction order. These are development
measurements, not an unseen-family or contamination-free evaluation.
